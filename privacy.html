Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

YT Channels Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by its owner, Uzair Akbar, to manage his own YouTube channels.

What data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests permission to upload videos to, and manage, the YouTube channel the owner selects. It reads basic channel information (channel name and ID) to confirm it is uploading to the right channel.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to upload and manage the owner's own videos.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never sent to any other server.
  • The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer any personal data to third parties.
  • The app does not access other users' data.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact
