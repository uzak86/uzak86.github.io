YT Channels Uploader

YT Channels Uploader is a small private tool used by Uzair Akbar to upload and schedule videos to his own YouTube channels:

  • Nightglass Sleep (@NightglassSleep)
  • Plimble (@Plimble)
  • Ledger & Tech (@LedgerAndTech)

It runs only on the owner's own computer and is not offered to the public. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and schedule publish times on channels the owner manages.
