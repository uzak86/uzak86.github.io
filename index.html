Akbar Video Uploader

Akbar Video Uploader is a small private tool used by Uzair Akbar to upload and schedule videos to his own YouTube channels:

  • Nightglass Sleep (@NightglassSleep)
  • Plimble (@Plimble)
  • Ledger & Tech (@LedgerAndTech)

It runs only on the owner's own computer and is not offered to the public. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and schedule publish times on channels the owner manages.

Works with YouTube: this tool uses YouTube API Services. Use of the tool is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
